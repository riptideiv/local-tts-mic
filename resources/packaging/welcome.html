Local TTS Mic

This installs the app in Applications and adds the Local TTS Mic virtual microphone.

Finish active calls and quit Local TTS Mic first. Installing the microphone briefly restarts all Mac audio.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac and macOS 15 or newer. Python and dependencies are included. On first launch, the app downloads several GB of speech models and example voices; allow at least 10 GB free. No Terminal, Git, Homebrew, or developer tools are needed.
